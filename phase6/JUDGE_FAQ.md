# Judge FAQ

This document anticipates technical and product questions from hackathon judges, strictly distinguishing between what is implemented, what is validated, and what is proposed for the future.

### 1. Why Snapdragon?
**[VALIDATED]** EdgePilot targets Snapdragon because its dedicated edge architecture allows MSMEs to run continuous, heavy visual inspection workloads on local devices rather than paying for cloud inference. 

### 2. Why NPU?
**[VALIDATED]** The Neural Processing Unit (NPU) is specifically designed to accelerate AI inference efficiently. We validated through Qualcomm AI Hub that our custom model executes on the Snapdragon Hexagon Tensor Processor (HTP/NPU), confirming the viability of hardware-accelerated edge computer vision.

### 3. Why YOLOv8-N?
**[IMPLEMENTED / VALIDATED]** YOLOv8-Nano is a lightweight, state-of-the-art object detection model. We chose it because it balances accuracy with the small memory footprint (4.73 MB) and fast latency (5.849 ms) required for practical edge deployment.

### 4. Why not train on the Snapdragon laptop?
**[PROPOSED/FUTURE]** For this prototype, model training was done separately to generate the PyTorch weights. In a future production scenario, the "Business Data Pipeline" could leverage cloud or dedicated local GPUs for the training phase, while the Snapdragon edge PC focuses entirely on optimized inference deployment.

### 5. Where was the model actually executed?
**[VALIDATED]** The performance numbers (5.849 ms, 4.73 MB) were generated by executing the custom ONNX model on a hosted Snapdragon X Elite CRD through the official Qualcomm AI Hub.

### 6. How do you know it used the NPU?
**[VALIDATED]** The Qualcomm AI Hub profile job explicitly returned hardware metrics confirming execution on the Hexagon Tensor Processor (HTP).

### 7. What does 5.849 ms mean?
**[VALIDATED]** This is the measured on-device inference latency for a single frame passing through our custom YOLOv8-N model on the Snapdragon X Elite NPU, exclusive of pre/post-processing overhead.

### 8. Why is local Mac inference slower?
**[IMPLEMENTED]** The Streamlit UI currently uses PyTorch running on the local Mac's CPU to demonstrate the functional end-to-end workflow (image upload → detection → business logic). It is a functional prototype and lacks the NPU hardware acceleration validated in our Qualcomm AI Hub tests.

### 9. Is this production-ready?
**[IMPLEMENTED]** No. EdgePilot is currently a proof-of-concept and validation prototype built to demonstrate the end-to-end viability of the concept.

### 10. How would an MSME provide its own data?
**[PROPOSED/FUTURE]** We have designed a "Business Data Pipeline" intended to let users upload custom manufacturing images, verify labels, and trigger an automated training pipeline. This is marked as "Coming Soon" in the current UI.

### 11. Does one model work for every industry?
**[PROPOSED/FUTURE]** No. EdgePilot's architecture assumes that a single generalized model is inefficient. Instead, the platform is designed to deploy *task-specific* models tailored to the exact configuration created by the business user.

### 12. How would EdgePilot support other inspection tasks?
**[PROPOSED/FUTURE]** By repeating the demonstrated workflow: ingesting task-specific data, fine-tuning a lightweight model, exporting it to ONNX, and deploying it to the edge hardware. The current UI shows how different tasks (e.g., Food Product Inspection) could be selected.

### 13. What happens if the business has a different defect type?
**[PROPOSED/FUTURE]** The business would provide images of the new defect type, which would be run through the proposed Business Data Pipeline to fine-tune a new task-specific iteration of the YOLOv8-N model.

### 14. What is implemented today versus future scope?
- **[IMPLEMENTED]** UI configuration, local PyTorch inference demonstration, business logic decoupling, custom model training (NEU-DET).
- **[VALIDATED]** Snapdragon X Elite NPU deployment via Qualcomm AI Hub.
- **[FUTURE]** Automatic user-data uploads, automated retraining, and direct on-device NPU inference integration within the Streamlit UI.

### 15. What is the role of Qualcomm AI Hub?
**[VALIDATED]** Qualcomm AI Hub was utilized to compile our custom `.pt` model into an optimized format, and then to profile that model directly on Snapdragon hardware to obtain our foundational validation metrics.
