# Competitive Positioning

This document defines the market positioning for EdgePilot.

*Note: This is our intended product positioning. We do not claim to be the "first," "only," or "unique" solution in the market, nor do we claim that no competitors exist.*

## EdgePilot's Focus

There are many existing and powerful tools in the AI ecosystem that provide:
- Model development
- Data annotation
- Model training
- Edge optimization
- Hardware deployment

**EdgePilot's intended focus is not to replace these individual tools, but to combine them into an MSME-oriented workflow.** 

EdgePilot focuses on the intersection of:
1. **Business Requirement Configuration:** Allowing a non-ML user to define what they are inspecting and what the business rules are.
2. **Task-Specific Visual AI:** Utilizing lightweight computer vision models dedicated to single tasks (like surface defect detection).
3. **Snapdragon-Specific Optimization & Deployment:** Ensuring the model actually runs efficiently on edge hardware using NPU acceleration.
4. **Business Decision Logic:** Translating raw bounding-box confidence scores directly into actionable outcomes ("PASS" or "REJECT").

By bridging the gap between high-level business rules and low-level NPU edge deployment, EdgePilot positions itself as an end-to-end automation platform for MSMEs who need practical visual inspection without building bespoke MLOps infrastructure.
